Laserfiche Cloud Platinum is the most prestigious level of the Laserfiche Certified Professional program, earned by completing multiple advanced sections that build on Gold-level expertise. It demonstrates the ability to design and deliver complex, end-to-end solutions in Laserfiche Cloud: advanced process automation and form design, integrations with external systems, sophisticated metadata and records management configurations, security and governance best practices, and solution optimization for scale. Platinum holders are recognized as top-tier experts capable of architecting enterprise-grade digital transformation projects, including solutions that take advantage of Laserfiche Cloud’s latest AI-driven capabilities.
