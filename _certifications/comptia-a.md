CompTIA A+ is the industry standard for establishing a career in IT. Earned by passing two exams (Core 1 and Core 2), it validates the ability to install, configure, maintain, and troubleshoot computer hardware, mobile devices, operating systems, and networks. It also covers security fundamentals, virtualization and cloud basics, and the professional practices expected in a support environment, such as documentation, change management, and clear communication with end users. It’s frequently required or preferred for help desk, desktop support, and field technician roles, and it meets the DoD 8140 baseline requirement for certain technical positions.
