Laserfiche Gold is the highest tier of the Laserfiche Certified Professional program, earned by completing a full track of technical courses and exams. It covers the platform end to end: capturing and organizing documents in the repository, designing electronic forms, building automated workflows, configuring metadata and records management, and administering the system, including users, security, and server configuration. Laserfiche 12 is the current release, focused on a redesigned interface and stronger administration built for scalability, so this certification shows up-to-date expertise in helping organizations go paperless and automate business processes.
