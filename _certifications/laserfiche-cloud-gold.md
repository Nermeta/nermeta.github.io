Laserfiche Cloud Gold is one of the highest tiers of the Laserfiche Certified Professional program for the platform’s cloud-hosted edition. It covers the full solution lifecycle in a SaaS environment: capturing and organizing documents in the cloud repository, designing electronic forms, building automated processes and workflows, configuring metadata, templates, and records management, and administering users, roles, and security through the cloud account. Because Laserfiche Cloud receives continuous updates, including AI-assisted features like Smart Fields and Smart Chat, this certification shows current expertise in helping organizations go paperless and automate business processes without managing on-premises infrastructure.
