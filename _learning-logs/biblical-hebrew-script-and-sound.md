## Why I'm Studying This
Languages have always been a personal fascination of mine. The idea that the language you speak has an effect on how you think and perceive the world around you has made me wonder what secrets are out there that I do not know because I only speak one language. So with that in mind why do I want to start with Biblical Hebrew of all languages? Well about 1/3 of the world's population believes in the Old Testament in one way or another. So much of the modern Western World has been influenced by the Bible. So I figure why not read the original? 

In addition, the advantage of studying ancient languages is that you do not need to worry about pronunciation all that much. Nobody really knows what it sounds like anyway so the intelligibility barrier in that respect is next to non existent. For me as an independent learner, this means I don't need to try and perfect my pronunciation. As long as I know what the word represents the noises that come out of my mouth are secondary.
